[Silverscreen Decoration & Fulfillment](/) has added three M&R machines: the HORNET automatic screen-printing press, the ORBITER automatic DTF application system, and the Eco-Rinse automatic screen-rinsing system to boost speed, quality, and capacity for the busy summer production season.

The summer season is one of the busiest stretches of the year for custom apparel. Events, team orders, festivals, and seasonal merchandise converge at once, and the shops that thrive are those built to keep pace. At Silverscreen Decoration & Fulfillment, we've prepared for the rush by investing in three new pieces of M&R equipment that strengthen every stage of our production process.

For over two decades, Silverscreen Decoration & Fulfillment has equipped businesses across the country with custom garment decoration and reliable fulfillment. We combine American craftsmanship with advanced technology, and our latest additions continue that commitment. Each machine was chosen with one goal in mind: to increase production capacity, improve quality, and increase capacity for the brands that trust us with their projects.

Here's a closer look at what's new on our production floor and what it means for your next order.

## What new equipment did Silverscreen add for the summer of 2026?

Silverscreen has added three M&R machines, each targeting a different part of the decoration workflow:

- [**M&R HORNET Automatic Screen Printing Press**](https://www.mrprint.com/equipment/hornet-automatic-screen-printing-press) — high-speed, high-quality screen printing
- [**M&R ORBITER Automatic DTF Application System**](https://www.mrprint.com/equipment/orbiter-automatic-direct-to-film-application-system) — fast, consistent heat transfer application
- [**M&R Eco-Rinse Automatic Screen Rinsing System**](https://www.mrprint.com/equipment/eco-rinse-automatic-screen-rinsing-system) — automated, water-saving screen processing

Together, these machines improve our screen printing, digital printing, and screen-making operations. Let's break down each one.

## How does the [M&R HORNET press](https://www.mrprint.com/equipment/hornet-automatic-screen-printing-press) improve screen printing?

The M&R HORNET is an automatic screen-printing press built for shops that demand reliability, speed, and consistency, such as [Silverscreen Decoration & Fulfillment](/).

### Key features of the HORNET

- **7.5-inch high-resolution touchscreen** with a clean icon system and multi-language tutorial screens built right into the display, so operators learn fast and rely less on external manuals.
- **Tool-free squeegee and floodbar angle adjustments** that speed up setup between jobs.
- **Front-flip screen holder with manual and pneumatic locks** for quick, secure screen loading.
- **Rear micro registration** for precise, repeatable adjustments.
- **Flat indexing** for smooth, quiet operation that keeps operators in rhythm.
- **Print speed up to 85 dozen per hour**.

What the HORNET means for your projects.

For Silverscreen clients, the HORNET translates into more efficient production and consistent print quality across every run. That repeatability matters most during the summer, when reorders, program work, and large event merchandise pile up quickly.

## How does the [M&R ORBITER](https://www.mrprint.com/equipment/orbiter-automatic-direct-to-film-application-system) expand our DTF capabilities?

The M&R ORBITER is a versatile rotary heat transfer machine designed for [Direct-to-Film (DTF) applications](/dtf). It draws on 40 years of M&R carousel engineering, adding the precision, pressure, and speed needed for optimal DTF transfers.

### Key features of the ORBITER

- **Rotary indexing base** that splits application time across multiple heat press platens to produce more than 360 transfers per hour under optimal conditions, well above the under-180 rate of a single manual press.
- **Auto-load and auto-peel stations** that remove repetitive manual tasks and reduce labor.
- **Full-color touchscreen HMI** with adjustable place and peel settings.
- **Robust, reinforced construction** built from heavy-duty steel and premium components.

The ORBITER turns passive "waiting" time into active pressing time, and it supports one-piece flow and Just-in-Time manufacturing.

### What the ORBITER means for your projects

DTF is ideal for complex, full-color designs and smaller custom runs. With the ORBITER, [Silverscreen Decoration & Fulfillmen](/)t can apply those transfers faster and more consistently, even on intricate artwork. The result is expanded DTF capacity and quicker delivery for the detailed, vibrant designs your summer campaigns demand.

## How does the [M&R Eco-Rinse](https://www.mrprint.com/equipment/eco-rinse-automatic-screen-rinsing-system) improve screen processing and sustainability?

Screen making is often the hidden bottleneck in a print shop. The M&R Eco-Rinse automates the tedious process of rinsing exposed screens, bringing consistency to a step that has traditionally depended on manual labor.

### Key features of the Eco-Rinse

- **Automated rinsing** that eliminates the human error tied to operator fatigue, boredom, or inadequate training.
- **Balanced pressure** from simultaneous sprays on both sides of the screen, which maintains screen tension and extends screen life.
- **Significant water savings.** Manual rinsing can use 10–15 liters (2.6–4.0 gallons) of water per screen, while Eco-Rinse typically uses less than half that amount and recycles water during the wash cycle to further reduce water use.
- **Large screen capacity,** accommodating one screen up to 60" x 60" x 2.25", or two screens within that combined limit.
- **Easy-to-use touchscreen** with M&R's Job Recall™, which stores up to five saved rinse sequences.
- **Stainless-steel construction** for durability and minimal maintenance.

The Eco-Rinse also processes screens in an enclosed environment, which reduces ambient humidity, a factor that can negatively affect film and emulsions across the entire shop.

### What does Eco-Rinse mean for your projects?

Faster, more consistent screen processing means we can move jobs from prep to press more quickly, with screens that last longer and perform better. Because Eco-Rinse cuts per-screen labor costs by up to half and slashes water consumption, it supports both efficiency and environmental responsibility. That's a win for your timeline and for the planet.
