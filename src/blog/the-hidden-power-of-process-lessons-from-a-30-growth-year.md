This week on the 2 Regular Guys Podcast, we’re joined by Daniel Frank, owner and CEO of Silverscreen Decoration & Fulfillment, to explore how process, leadership, and strategic outsourcing can completely transform a growing business. With more than 30 years of experience in logistics and manufacturing, Dan has seen firsthand how disciplined systems and a solutions-first mindset drive real, measurable growth. His company is up 30% this year, with monthly volume more than doubling. Proof that process can outperform hustle. Daniel shares how outsourcing marketing, IT, art, and finance actually strengthened his company’s control and culture, and how focusing on client-driven solutions led to new revenue streams and loyal partnerships. If you’ve ever wondered how to scale without losing your sanity, this conversation is packed with insight and inspiration.
