### Unveiling "The Kraken": A Revolution in Color and Speed

The Kraken is a comprehensive DTF printing system engineered for those who demand the absolute pinnacle of quality and output. This machine fundamentally changes what is possible in custom apparel decoration through a unique combination of expanded color capabilities and raw production speed.

### Industry-First 9-Color Expanded Gamut

Most commercial printers utilize a standard four-color process. The Kraken introduces an industry-first 9-color expanded gamut configuration. By utilizing Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Black, and White, alongside Red, Green, Orange, and a proprietary "Super Blue," this system achieves a 35% increase in available color gamut. For our customers, this means we can match your corporate colors and branding guidelines with unprecedented accuracy.

### Unmatched Production Speeds

High-volume orders require equipment that can keep up with strict corporate deadlines. The Kraken is equipped with eight Epson i3200 print heads, allowing it to command serious output without compromising image clarity. In its standard six-pass production mode, the system comfortably prints over 400 square feet per hour.

### Precision Engineering for Large Orders

Stability is critical when running massive printing jobs. The Kraken utilizes a Magnetic Rail Drive and a Dual Main Beam to ensure flawless precision across its expansive 32-inch (80 CM) roll media width. This robust construction ensures that every print is identical, from the first to the last, delivering consistent, high-quality results for intricate designs across your entire order.

### Introducing "The Sentinel": Precision and Automation in Cutting

Printing the transfers is only the first half of the DTF process. Traditionally, cutting these transfers required extensive manual labor, introducing the potential for human error and bottlenecking production. We have eliminated these operational hurdles with the integration of The Sentinel.

### Advanced Camera Vision

The Sentinel is the industry’s first vision-based, high-volume DTF cutter. It utilizes advanced camera vision to automatically identify and contour-cut printed transfers. It seamlessly tracks the edges of complex designs, delivering flawlessly cut graphics at industrial speeds.

### Intelligent "Gangify" Technology

This automated cutter pairs perfectly with our proprietary "Gangify" technology. The software intelligently groups multiple designs together on a single roll of film, and The Sentinel reads the exact layout to execute the cuts. This advanced integration allows the machine to automatically detect and separate different graphics, enabling hands-free operation.

### Nonstop Performance for Rapid Scaling

The Sentinel is built for continuous, day-and-night operation. By automating the cutting process, we drastically reduce production time and eliminate operator fatigue. For our enterprise clients, this means we can move your projects faster, handle higher volumes, and reduce waste, so your international markets receive your products on time.

### The Synergistic Power: Kraken + Sentinel = Unrivaled DTF Production

When paired together, The Kraken and The Sentinel create a fully automated, end-to-end DTF production line. The workflow transitions seamlessly from the vibrant, high-speed output of the 9-color printer directly into the intelligent, vision-guided finishing of the automated cutter.

This integration completely removes the traditional friction points of apparel decoration. The combined system automatically identifies alignment markers, processes complex batches without manual intervention, and produces consistent, retail-ready transfers. By running these machines together, Silverscreen Decoration & Fulfillment delivers faster, more reliable delivery times for your initiatives.
