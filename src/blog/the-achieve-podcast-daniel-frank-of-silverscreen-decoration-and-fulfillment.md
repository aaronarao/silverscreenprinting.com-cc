In this episode, Host Taylor Baker sits down with Dan Frank, CEO of Silver Screen Decoration and Fulfillment, to discuss how a modern apparel decoration company scales by pairing production capabilities with fulfillment, systems, and smart use of emerging tools like AI.

What You’ll Learn

• Why “innovation” in this industry means expanding beyond one decoration method (screen print, embroidery, digital, patches, sewing) to meet customers wherever they are.

• How adding fulfillment (inventory management, pick/pack/ship, distribution) turns printing into a complete operational solution for brands.

• The strategic value of “all-in” service bundles even when individual pieces aren’t high-margin because they increase retention and make relationships stickier.

• What changes when a business jumps from a few million in revenue to aggressive growth targets, and why clear roles and accountability become non-negotiable.

• How to approach leadership team building: getting the right people in the right seats (and making hard decisions when they’re not).

• Practical ways AI can improve B2B pipeline development and sales execution, from prospect research to accelerating RFQ responses.

• Lessons from past boom-and-bust cycles: pacing growth, avoiding overextension, and building a healthier financial foundation.

• Why scalable operating systems and software infrastructure often become the biggest constraint as demand accelerates.

Dan’s story highlights the difference between simply producing a great product and building a company designed to scale one that combines operational breadth, disciplined leadership, and continuous learning. By moving beyond “printing” into end-to-end solutions (and actively embracing modern tools), Silver Screen positions itself as a long-term partner to brands that need reliability, speed, and a better customer experience as they grow.
